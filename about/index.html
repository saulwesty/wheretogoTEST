About Where To Go

Written and maintained by SaulLast updated Sept 2026

Why this exists

London has more competitive socialising venues than anyone can keep track of, and almost none of them make it easy to find out what an evening will actually involve. Details sit three clicks into a booking flow, or get left out of the venue's own marketing entirely.

Meanwhile the round-ups that rank highest in search are mostly rewritten press releases, not first-hand research.

So the premise is narrow and, we hope, useful: find out what these places are actually like, and say plainly who each one suits.

Corrections

Venues change, and we get things wrong. If something on this site is out of date or inaccurate, tell us and we will fix it and note the change on the page.

Report a correction

Who writes this

Saul writes and researches every page on Where To Go. His background is in sports, technology and marketing.
