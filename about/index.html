About Where To Go London

Written and maintained by SaulLast updated October 2026

Where To Go London is the ultimate independent guide to competitive socialising venues in London, with verified prices and comprehensive comparisons. It was started by Jonny and Saul, and every venue is compared on price, time of day, the group it suits best and the type of activity.

Where did Where To Go London come from?

Where To Go London was started by Jonny and Saul, two Londoners who were always trying to find fun things to do in the city. They enjoyed competitive socialising and activity bars, and they suspected that many of the best venues were still undiscovered. They also noticed that no single directory covered all of these venues or explained which suited different occasions, so they built one.

What does Where To Go London do?

Where To Go London aims to be the best place to compare competitive socialising venues in London and decide between them. Every venue is compared on the same four things:

  • Price
  • Time of day
  • The group it suits best
  • Type of activity

The guide is useful for planning office socials and for groups of friends meeting up. Where To Go London is the most comprehensive and accurate place to find activities to do in London, and no other directory has found and compared all of them.

How does Where To Go London research venues?

Every fact is checked against the venue's own website at the time of publishing. Where To Go London is independent, and anything sponsored is clearly labelled as sponsored. Pages are kept up to date regularly, so prices and details stay current.

What does Where To Go London stand for?

Three things guide every page:

  • Clear and straightforward. We use plain language and full sentences, with no unexplained jargon.
  • Helpful. Every page helps someone decide where to go and what to book.
  • Honest. Anything sponsored is clearly labelled as sponsored.

Who is Where To Go London for?

Where To Go London is for groups of friends socialising, office managers organising socials, couples planning a date and families organising activities.

Corrections

Venues change, and we get things wrong. If something on this site is out of date or inaccurate, tell us and we will fix it and note the change on the page.

Report a correction

Who writes this

Saul is in charge of data accuracy at Where To Go London. He checks every price and detail against the venue’s own website and leads the site’s technical SEO, so the guide is accurate, covers as much of London as possible and is easy to find.

Jonny looks after partnerships and growth. He works with venues and activity providers so that more people can find and book great nights out in London.
