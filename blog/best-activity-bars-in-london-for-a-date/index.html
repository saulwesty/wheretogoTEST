What are the best activity bars in London for a date?

SWritten by SaulPublished 26 October 2026Last verified September 2026

The short answer

The best activity bars for a date in London give you a game to play side by side, which takes the pressure off the conversation. Boulebar, Draughts and Swingers suit a first date, Electric Shuffle, Puttshack and NQ64 suit a second or third, and Rowans suits a relaxed daytime date. Check the age rules and booking terms below before you go.

01

Boulebar, South Bank

From £10per person for 90 minutes on a court

Boulebar is a French-style bar built around pétanque, a game where you throw metal balls (boules) as close as you can to a small target ball. You book a court for 90 minutes with all the equipment included, and each court takes 2 to 6 players, so a couple gets one to themselves. Eighteen of the 20 courts are outdoors, behind the National Theatre, with covered courts in winter, and you play with a drink in hand.

Why it works for a date

Best forA first date, especially on a weekend afternoon.
On the dateYou stand side by side and take turns, so there is always something to talk about. The kitchen serves French classics and sharing charcuterie boards, with French beers, ciders and cocktails.
Worth knowingBoulebar is open 3pm to 11pm Monday to Friday, noon to 11pm on Saturday and noon to 6pm on Sunday. It is cashless, dogs are welcome, and it is closed from 24 December to 4 January. Walk-ins are accepted when a court is free, but booking is recommended at busy times.

02

Draughts, Waterloo

From £7.95per person, midweek before 4pm (£9.95 at peak)

Draughts is a board game café and bar with more than 1,000 games to choose from, in a railway arch in the Leake Street tunnel beneath Waterloo station. A table is held for up to three hours for a per-person fee, and staff called Game Gurus recommend a game for your group and teach you the rules. A full kitchen and a bar run alongside the games, with vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options.

Why it works for a date

Best forA first date, or a lazy afternoon.
On the dateYou choose how competitive it gets, from a quick card game to a long strategy game, and nobody has to pretend to know the rules.
Worth knowingOff-peak is midweek from opening until 4pm, and peak is weekday evenings, weekends and bank holidays. Tables sell out at peak times, so book ahead. Outside food and drink is not allowed. Draughts is open noon to 11pm Monday to Thursday, noon to midnight on Friday, 11am to midnight on Saturday and 11am to 11pm on Sunday.

03

Swingers, West End

From £12per person for crazy golf

Swingers is a nine-hole crazy golf course styled as a 1920s English seaside resort, a minute’s walk from Oxford Circus. A round takes 30 to 40 minutes at busy times, and next to the course is Carnival, a retro arcade with more than 30 games. Four cocktail bars and a rotating line-up of street-food traders, including Pizza Pilgrims, Patty & Bun and Breddos Tacos, mean you can eat and drink as you play.

Why it works for a date

Best forA first or second date.
On the dateGolf gives you a light, shared challenge, and Carnival gives you somewhere to carry on afterwards. The Swingers Experience at £19 each adds 30 minutes of Carnival, and the Twin Set at £35 each adds Carnival and a £20 drink credit.
Worth knowingAfter 6pm Swingers is an over-18s venue with a Challenge 25 policy, so bring photo ID. Tickets are released two months ahead, are non-refundable and cannot be changed within 48 hours of the booking. Swingers is cashless.

04

Electric Shuffle, London Bridge

From £10per person for 60 minutes, Sunday to Tuesday

Electric Shuffle is a shuffleboard bar, where you slide weighted pucks down a long table and a projection system runs the games and scores every shot automatically. You book 60 or 90 minutes in a semi-private area, and food and drink are ordered with a call button on the scoreboard, so you never need to leave the game. The sourdough pizzas are the standout on the menu, alongside cocktails, burgers and small plates.

Why it works for a date

Best forA second date or later, when you are happy to be a little competitive.
On the dateThe table explains each game as you play and keeps the score, so you can concentrate on each other.
Worth knowingEveryone must be 18 or over to play, even with an adult present. Saturday bookings need a group of at least 6, so a couple cannot book a Saturday. You can cancel or postpone up to 48 hours before, and after that the gaming fee is not refunded.

05

Puttshack, Bank

From £14per person for a 9-hole round

Puttshack is crazy golf where the ball does the scoring: each ball carries a chip that sensors under the green track, so your score appears on a screen without anyone writing it down. Bank has three 9-hole courses and three bars, and a round for two players takes about 30 minutes. A full kitchen and cocktail bar run the length of the venue, including the signature Sapphire Island Iced Tea.

Why it works for a date

Best forA second date, or a first date on a weekday.
On the dateSome holes are themed games, such as a beer-pong hole and a Connect Four hole, which gives you something to laugh about.
Worth knowingReviewers report an on-screen timer and waits of 15 to 20 minutes per hole at busy times, so a weekday or early evening is calmer. A 10% service charge is added when you book online. Unlimited golf is available Sunday to Thursday only, and full payment is taken at booking.

06

NQ64, Soho

From Free entrytokens from £10.50 for 15

NQ64 is a retro arcade bar with more than 60 arcade cabinets across two floors at Orange Yard, plus pool tables, interactive dartboards and retro consoles that are free to play. Cabinets run on tokens, which cost £10.50 for 15 or £14 for 25, and most take one or two tokens a play. Soho is NQ64’s biggest site, opened in September 2026, with a cocktail bar built around the games.

Why it works for a date

Best forA second or third date, or a later start to the evening.
On the dateYou can play side by side on the free consoles or compete on the cabinets, and move between the two floors as the night goes on.
Worth knowingEntry is free, except after 9pm on Thursday and Friday and after 8pm on Saturday, when a door charge applies. NQ64 is open 3pm to 2am Monday to Thursday, 3pm to 3am on Friday and noon to 3am on Saturday. General entry is walk-in only, and the venue is a couple of minutes from Tottenham Court Road station.

07

Rowans, Finsbury Park

From £9.60per person for one game with admission, Monday to Friday before 6pm

Rowans is a bowling alley in Finsbury Park with 24 lanes across two floors, plus karaoke booths, pool tables and a bar in the same building. It has been open since 1988, and it is a no-frills bowling alley rather than a cocktail bar, so our guide rates it lower for dates than the other venues here. Two games takes roughly 45 minutes.

Why it works for a date

Best forA relaxed daytime or early-evening date.
On the dateBowling gives you a turn each and time to talk in between, and pool or karaoke are there if you want to carry on.
Worth knowingBowling is charged per game with shoe hire included, plus an admission fee of £1, rising to £5 on Friday and Saturday after 7pm. After 7pm Rowans is for over-21s only, with photo ID checked at the door. The venue has a long reputation as a cash-heavy place, so bring some cash as well as a card.

How do you choose an activity bar for a date?

Think about how well you know each other

On a first date, a game you can play side by side keeps things light and gives you something to talk about. Boulebar, Draughts and Swingers all work that way. On a second or third date, Electric Shuffle, Puttshack and NQ64 add more competition, which suits two people who already feel comfortable teasing each other.

First date

Boulebar, Draughts and SwingersEach one gives you a light game to play side by side.

Second or third date

Electric Shuffle, Puttshack and NQ64These are more competitive, and the scoring is automatic.

Daytime

Draughts and RowansDraughts opens at noon on weekdays and Rowans at 10:30am.

Late night

NQ64It is open until 2am Monday to Thursday and 3am on Friday and Saturday.

Think about whether you can talk

We have not measured noise levels at these venues, so we cannot give you a decibel figure. Reviewers describe weekday afternoons and early evenings as calmer than weekend nights at most activity bars, so go earlier in the week if being able to hear each other matters to you.

What should you check before you book?

Age rules

Electric Shuffle is 18 and over for everyone who plays. Swingers is over-18s only after 6pm and asks for photo ID under its Challenge 25 policy. Rowans is for over-21s only after 7pm, with photo ID checked at the door.

Group size and booking terms

Electric Shuffle needs a group of at least 6 on a Saturday, so a couple should book another day. Swingers tickets are non-refundable and cannot be changed within 48 hours of the booking, and Electric Shuffle allows cancellations up to 48 hours before. Boulebar and Draughts recommend booking at busy times, and NQ64 takes walk-ins for general entry.

Payment

Boulebar and Swingers are cashless. Rowans has a long reputation as a cash-heavy venue, so bring some cash as well as a card.

Prices and rules change. Check the venue’s own booking page before you book.
