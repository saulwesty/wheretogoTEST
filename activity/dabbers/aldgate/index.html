What is Dabbers Social Bingo, Aldgate?

Dabbers Social Bingo, Aldgate is a purpose-built bingo hall running a changing lineup of themed shows, hosted by comedians and performers rather than a traditional caller. Food comes from Christy's Kitchen, covering burgers, chicken strips, nachos and sharing plates, alongside a separate cocktail and shots menu.

How does it work?

Each ticket includes entertainment, a bingo card, a dabber and a seat at a shared table for the show; you're not booking an exclusive space unless you take on full private hire. Shows run most nights of the week, each with its own theme and price, and a separate bottomless brunch format runs on Saturday afternoons with two rounds of bingo alongside bottomless drinks.

Age policy

Dabbers is an over-18s venue for every regular show. The exception is Family Brunch Bingo, a separate, dedicated event where children are welcome.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

18-22 Houndsditch, London EC3A 7DB. Aldgate station is about two minutes on foot, on the Circle and Metropolitan lines. Liverpool Street, with mainline and further Underground connections, is five to eight minutes away.

How do you book?

Tickets for individual shows are booked online by date.

Ticket prices vary by show and date, from around £13.20 per person on quieter weekday nights up to about £25 for the busiest themed shows. Tickets include a shared table, entertainment, a bingo card and a dabber.
