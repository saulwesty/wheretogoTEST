What is Mission: Breakout, Kentish Town?

Mission: Breakout runs three escape rooms inside the old South Kentish Town station, closed to passengers since 1924: Codebreakers, The Lost Passenger and UNDER-GROUND 2099.

Each game is a private room for your team, with no mixing with other players.

How does it work?

You book one of the three games for your team and have 60 minutes to escape. Rooms take 3 to 6 players, and all three rooms can run side by side for groups of up to 18.

Games suit ages 9 and up; under-15s must be with an adult.

What is the food and drink like?

There's no food on site; Kentish Town Road and Camden Town are close by for before or after.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

141-145 Kentish Town Road, NW1 8PB. Kentish Town and Camden Town stations, on the Northern line, are both a short walk.

How do you book?

Book online through Mission: Breakout's own site. Groups larger than 6 can book several rooms at the same time.
