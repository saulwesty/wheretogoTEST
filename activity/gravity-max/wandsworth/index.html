What is Gravity Max, Wandsworth?

Gravity Max is an indoor entertainment centre in Southside, Wandsworth, with thirteen activities under one roof: indoor karting, ten-pin bowling, AR darts, urban street golf, shuffleboard, beer pong, Howzat batting cages, a Pixel Hyperfloor, the Hologate VR Arena and Tower-Tag, Sandbox VR, an Immersive Gamebox and an arcade.

There are bars and food spots on site, with a sports bar and live music.

How does it work?

Book activities individually, or combine three with a MAX3 Pass. There's also a Wednesday Play Pass and a MAX Family Pass.

Everyone completes a safety agreement before playing. Karting prices vary by session; check them when booking.

There are several food spots and bars, including a sports bar with live music.

How do you get there?

Southside, Wandsworth High Street, SW18 4TF. Wandsworth Town station is about ten minutes' walk.

How do you book?

Book online through Gravity's own site. Student and Blue Light discounts are available online.
