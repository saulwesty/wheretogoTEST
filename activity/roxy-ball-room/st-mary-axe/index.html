What is Roxy Ball Room, St Mary Axe?

Roxy Ball Room, St Mary Axe is a multi-activity games bar next to the Gherkin. It runs nine bookable activities: duck pin bowling, American pool, ping pong, tech darts, tech shuffle, karaoke, crazy pool, ice-free curling and shooting pods, plus beer pong on application. Activities can be booked individually or mixed together.

Food is American-style: pizzas, burgers and wings, with a full bar of cocktails, draught beer, cider and mocktails alongside.

How does it work?

Duck pin bowling and crazy pool are priced per person, per game. Karaoke, tech darts, tech shuffle, ice-free curling and shooting pods are priced per person, per hour. American pool and ping pong are priced per table, per hour, so the cost splits further across a bigger group.

Booking two or three activities together through Mix & Match brings the per-person price down. The activity you want most has to be the one you book first, since that's what unlocks the discount on the rest. There's a cap of 10 people per pool table, shuffleboard, ping pong table or tech darts board, and karaoke on Saturdays needs a minimum of four people. Mix & Match bookings run for groups of two to thirty.

How much does it cost for one activity at Roxy Ball Room, St Mary Axe?

£12 for most activities, or £24 per table for American pool or ping pong.

What is the food and drink like?

Food covers American-style pizzas, burgers and chicken wings, with sides to share. The bar has cocktails, draught beer and cider, and mocktails alongside soft drinks. The full menu is on Roxy Ball Room's own menu page.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

33 St Mary Axe, Unit 6, London EC3A 8AA. Right next to the Gherkin, a short walk from both Aldgate and Bank stations.

How do you book?

Bookings for individual activities and Mix & Match packages go through Roxy's online booking system, or by phone. Decide on your priority activity before you start, since it's the first one you book that qualifies the visit for the Mix & Match discount.
