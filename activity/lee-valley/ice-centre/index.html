What is Lee Valley Ice Centre, Leyton?

Lee Valley Ice Centre on Lea Bridge Road has two Olympic-size ice rinks under one roof, one of the largest skating venues in the south of England. Public skating sessions run every day, alongside ice discos, morning Coffee Skate sessions and birthday parties.

It's a leisure centre rather than a bar, with a gym, studios and a café on site.

How does it work?

Book a public skating session, or an ice disco for something livelier. Skate hire is £3 on top of the session price, and skate aids are £5.

In the school holidays, prices rise and family tickets are available for two adults and two children, or one adult and three children.

How much is skating at Lee Valley Ice Centre?

£13 per adult and £10 per junior, plus £3 skate hire; £16 and £13 in the school holidays.

What is the food and drink like?

There's a café on site, open daily from 10am to 5:30pm.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

Lea Bridge Road, E10 7QL. Lea Bridge and Clapton stations are both nearby. There are 155 parking spaces, with up to four hours free.
