What is Immersive Gamebox, Southbank?

Immersive Gamebox puts your group in a private room with floor-to-ceiling screens, motion tracking and touch-sensitive walls, then runs team games across it. Southbank is in a railway arch on Scoresby Street, with 13 boxes.

Games range from the officially licensed Squid Game and Ghostbusters to Paw Patrol for younger players.

How does it work?

You book a box for 30 or 60 minutes and play through a game of your choice as a team of up to 6. Up to 78 people can play at once across all the boxes.

Prices vary by day and time; peak covers weekends, school holidays and busier weekday evenings.

How much is Immersive Gamebox, Southbank?

From £30 per person for an hour of games, or £42 to £69 for packages with drinks and food.

What is the food and drink like?

Drinks are sold during play, and packages add drinks, a private area and pizza. Kids' parties include pizza, fries and squash.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

Arch 3, 83 Scoresby Street, SE1 0XN. Southwark station, on the Jubilee line, is a few minutes' walk.

How do you book?

Book online through Immersive Gamebox's own site, choosing a game, a length and a time. Kids' parties, corporate events and monthly Game Passes are also available.
