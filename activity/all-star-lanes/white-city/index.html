What is All Star Lanes, White City?

All Star Lanes is retro-styled boutique bowling with an American diner menu and cocktail bars. White City has eight lanes, two cocktail bars, classic arcade games, private karaoke booths and an outdoor terrace.

It's one of four London All Star Lanes, alongside Brick Lane, Holborn and Stratford.

How does it work?

Each lane takes up to 7 people; bigger groups are split across lanes. Off-peak here is all day Monday and Tuesday, and before 4pm Wednesday to Friday; peak is the rest of the week.

Booking ahead carries a £7 per person minimum spend on food or drink, on top of the game price.

How much is bowling at All Star Lanes, White City?

£10 per adult per game off-peak or £14 at peak, and £9.50 for children.

What is the food and drink like?

An American diner menu runs alongside the lanes, with cocktails from the bar.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

Westfield London, 1081 Ariel Way, W12 7GF. White City and Wood Lane stations are both a short walk, and Shepherd's Bush is just beyond.

How do you book?

Book a lane online through All Star Lanes' own site. Advance bookings carry a £7 per person minimum spend on food or drink.
