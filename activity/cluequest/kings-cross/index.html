What is clueQuest, King's Cross?

clueQuest is one of the UK's longest-running escape room companies, with six spy-themed missions on Caledonian Road near King's Cross, including Plan52, Operation BlackSheep, IAMAI and the 18+ Post Mortem.

Each mission is a private room for your team.

How does it work?

You book a mission for your team and have 60 minutes to solve it. Team sizes vary by mission, from 2 to 7 on some and 4 to 8 on others.

Most missions suit all ages; Revenge of the Sheep and cQ Origenes are 14+, and Post Mortem is 18+.

How much is clueQuest?

£31.99 per person off-peak or £36.99 at peak, so a team of four pays about £128 to £148.

What is the food and drink like?

clueQuest doesn't serve food; Caledonian Road and King's Cross have plenty of places to eat nearby.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

169-171 Caledonian Road, N1 0SL. King's Cross St Pancras is about a 10-minute walk.

How do you book?

Book a mission and a time slot online through clueQuest's own site. Student and group deals are available.
