Bowling and games · Victoria

Lane7, Victoria

  • Bowling from £11.50pp off-peak, standalone.
  • Bundle in more games for £13pp (two), £18pp (three) or £21pp (four).
  • Also has mini golf, shuffleboard, darts and an 18+ arcade hall downstairs.

What is Lane7?

Lane7 is a boutique bowling chain that bundles bowling together with a stack of other games under one roof: mini golf, shuffleboard, classic and augmented-reality darts and beer pong, plus Play Dirty, a secret basement arcade hall for adults with carnival-style games.

Victoria is inside the Zig Zag Building, a short walk from the station, with a full bar and kitchen running throughout.

How does it work?

Bowling is priced per person and can be booked on its own. Booking it alongside one or more of Lane7's other games as a package brings the per-person price down, similar to a mix-and-match deal.

Play Dirty, the basement arcade hall, is over-18s only and runs as a separate experience from the main bowling and games floor.

How much does it cost to bowl at Lane7, Victoria?

From £11.50 per person off-peak, for bowling alone.

Is it cheaper to book more than one game?

Per activity, yes: two games works out to £6.50pp per game, against £11.50pp for bowling alone.

What is the food and drink like?

The kitchen runs burgers and sharing platters, with a full bar of cocktails, craft beer, draught and cider. Happy Hour runs Monday to Friday, 4pm to 8pm: 2-for-1 cocktails and £5 pints, wine and spirits.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

Zig Zag Building, 70 Victoria Street, SW1E 6SQ. About a 5 to 10 minute walk from Victoria station, which sits on the Victoria and District & Circle lines plus National Rail.

How do you book?

Book online through Lane7's own booking system, choosing bowling alone or a games package and a time slot. Walk-ins are accepted but advance booking is recommended, particularly on Friday and Saturday evenings.
