What is Boulebar, Spitalfields?

Boulebar is pétanque with a French bar attached: you book a court, pick up the boules and play with a drink in hand. Spitalfields is underground on Frying Pan Alley, with ten indoor courts, a French restaurant and a Parisian cocktail bar.

There are two London Boulebars, at South Bank and Spitalfields.

How does it work?

You book a court for 90 minutes for a group of 2 to 6, and all equipment is included. Walk-ins are accepted when a court is free, but booking is recommended at busy times.

Spitalfields is fully indoors, so it plays the same whatever the weather.

What is the food and drink like?

The kitchen serves French classics and sharing charcuterie boards, with French beers, cidres and cocktails. The venue is cashless.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

7 Frying Pan Alley, E1 7HS. Liverpool Street station is about five minutes' walk.

How do you book?

Book a court online through Boulebar's own site. Team building, events and private hire go through the events team.
