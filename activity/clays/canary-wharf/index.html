What is Clays, Canary Wharf?

Clays is virtual clay pigeon shooting: a motion-tracked, shotgun-style controller fires at clays projected on a screen, with the system scoring every shot. Canary Wharf is the waterside site on One Cabot Square, with semi-private pegs, a private shooting room and a heated outdoor terrace.

It's one of the London Clays sites alongside Soho and the City.

How does it work?

You book a peg for your group and shoot through a set of scored games against a shared leaderboard. Session length depends on group size: one or two players get 40 minutes, three to five get an hour, and six to 22 get 90 minutes, all at the same price each.

Clays hasn't published an age policy for Canary Wharf; Clays Soho is 18+, so check before bringing under-18s.

How much is Clays, Canary Wharf?

£10 per person for a standard booking, with a longer session the bigger your group.

What is the food and drink like?

There's a full bar and food menu, with al fresco dining on the heated terrace by the water. Clays also runs seasonal packages such as Bottoms Up and Night Play.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

Unit B1, One Cabot Square, E14 4QJ. Canary Wharf station is about two minutes' walk, on the Jubilee and Elizabeth lines and the DLR.

How do you book?

Book online through Clays' own site. The private room and large-group packages go through the events team.
