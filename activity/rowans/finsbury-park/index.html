Bowling · Finsbury Park

Rowans, Finsbury Park

  • Bowling costs £8.60 to £10.80 per person per game, plus a £1 or £5 admission fee on top.
  • Over-21s only after 7pm, with photo ID checked at the door.
  • The building also has karaoke booths, pool tables and a bar.

What is Rowans?

Rowans is a bowling alley in Finsbury Park with 24 lanes across two floors. There's a bar and simple food alongside the lanes, and the same building has karaoke booths, pool tables and a dance floor for later in the evening.

How does it work?

Bowling is priced per game rather than per hour, and there's a separate admission fee on top, covered in the pricing section below. Two games is a typical night out, taking roughly 45 minutes for a normal-sized group.

Age policy

Rowans is over-21s only after 7pm, and photo ID is checked at the door. Under-10s get free admission on weekdays before 6pm if accompanied by an adult, though bowling itself is still charged, and under-12s are never admitted without an adult at any time. Once the evening session begins, under-18s must leave by 10pm from Sunday to Thursday, or by 7.30pm on Friday and Saturday.

What you actually pay

  • Thursday evening, one game: £9.70 plus £1 admission, so £10.70. Two games is £19.30.
  • Friday evening, two games: £21.60 plus £5 admission, so £26.60.
  • Monday afternoon, two games: £17.20 plus £1, so £18.20.
  • For twelve people, two games on a Thursday evening: around £232.

The Friday admission jump is the thing to plan around. £5 a head on top of a higher game rate means a Friday night costs nearly 40% more than the same two games on a Thursday. For a team of twelve that is close to £90 of difference.

What else is there

Karaoke, pool and a dance floor are also on site, in the same building. Karaoke and pool are priced separately from bowling, and we have not sampled either rate yet.

What is the food and drink like?

Food covers burgers, hot dogs, fish fingers and chicken goujons, with a kids' menu, sides and sauces. The bar has draught and bottled beer, wines, spirits, frozen cocktails and soft drinks.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

Finsbury Park. Victoria line, Piccadilly line and Great Northern mainline services all stop there. Finsbury Park station is step-free.

How do you book?

Group bookings can be made through Rowans' website, where deposits are taken by card. Photo ID is checked at the door after 7pm, so bring it if you're visiting in the evening.

Common questions

How much is bowling at Rowans?
Between £8.60 and £10.80 per person per game, plus an admission fee of £1 or £5. A Thursday evening game works out at £10.70 all in.
Is there an age limit at Rowans?
Yes. It is over-21s only after 19:00 and photo ID is required. Under-18s must leave by 22:00 Sunday to Thursday and by 19:30 on Friday and Saturday.
What is the admission fee?
£1 at most times, rising to £5 per person on Friday and Saturday after 19:00. It is charged on top of the game price.
Is shoe hire included?
Yes, shoe hire is included in the game price.
How many games should we book?
Two is a normal evening and takes roughly 45 minutes for an average group.
What else is there to do?
Karaoke, pool, food, and music and dancing, all on the same site. Prices for those are not yet confirmed.
