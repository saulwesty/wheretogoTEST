Flight Club, Victoria

What is Flight Club?

Flight Club is an award-winning darts venue. Each group gets its own throwing area, with a dartboard, a screen and sensors that score every throw automatically, running short arcade-style games rather than a traditional 501 match. It works as well for people who play regularly as for people who've never thrown a dart. Scores build into a running leaderboard across the group, and a highlight reel of your best throws is sent to you after the session.

Food and drink come to your area, so no one has to break off from the group to queue at a bar.

Flight Club has four sites across London: Bloomsbury, Shoreditch, Victoria and Islington, with one published price list across all of them. Victoria is the largest of the four, holding up to 650 guests, and it also has an outdoor terrace called The Magic Gardens, though darts aren't played out there.

How does it work?

Each booking gets its own throwing area for 60 or 90 minutes, shared by the whole group. It suits groups of any size, from a small group of friends to a full office social, with an events team stepping in for larger bookings. Games are mostly played in pairs, so everyone in the group gets a turn.

How much is Flight Club Victoria from Sunday to Tuesday?

From Sunday to Tuesday, a table costs £10 per person for 60 minutes or £14 per person for 90 minutes.

How much is Flight Club Victoria from Wednesday to Saturday?

From Wednesday to Saturday, a table costs £13 per person for 60 minutes or £17 per person for 90 minutes.

Social Darts bookings need a minimum group size from 4pm on weekdays and all day Saturday: four guests from 4pm Monday and Tuesday, rising to six guests from 4pm Wednesday to Friday and all day Saturday. Groups of up to around 12 tend to work best, since everyone gets a good number of turns in the time booked; larger groups can book a second throwing area to keep the game moving.

What is the food and drink like?

Food is sharing-style bar food: wings, tacos, sliders, sticks and a sharing platter, plus pizzas and sides, with vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options throughout. Drinks cover draught beer and cider, a full cocktail list, wine and champagne by the glass or bottle, and a low-and-no-alcohol range. The full menu is on Flight Club's bar and kitchen page.

How expensive are drinks at Flight Club Victoria?

Beers start from £6.70 and wines start from £6.80.

How much should I expect to spend at Flight Club Victoria?

As a rough guide, an hour of Social Darts at peak times, two pints and a share of two sides works out at around £30 per person.

How do you get there?

Victoria, on the Victoria, District and Circle lines plus the mainline station, is a short walk from the venue: the easiest Flight Club for anyone coming from the south or west.

How do you book?

Booking happens online: pick a site, a date, a session length and headcount. Booking ahead is worth doing, especially at weekends.
