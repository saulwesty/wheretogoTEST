What is Flight Club, Bloomsbury?

Flight Club is social darts: automatic scoring, arcade-style games and your own oche, rather than a proper 501 match. Bloomsbury spreads over two floors with stained-glass windows, nine semi-private oches and a carousel bar that Flight Club says is the only one of its kind in London.

It's one of four London Flight Clubs, alongside Shoreditch, Victoria and Islington, and all four charge the same prices.

How does it work?

You book an oche for 60 or 90 minutes and share it with your group, while sensors track every throw and keep score. Most games are played in pairs, so everyone gets a turn, and nobody needs to be good at darts.

Flight Club runs dedicated Family Sessions here: anyone aged 12 or over can play with an adult, Sunday to Friday until 4:30pm.

What is the food and drink like?

Food is Flight Club's sharing-style bar menu: wings, tacos, sliders, pizzas and sides, brought to your oche while you play, with vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free options. Drinks run from draught beer to sharing cocktails, served from the bars including the carousel bar. The full menu is on Flight Club's own bar and kitchen page.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

55 New Oxford Street, WC1A 1BS. Tottenham Court Road and Holborn stations are both a few minutes' walk.

How do you book?

Book online through Flight Club's own site, choosing a date, a time and a group size. Evening and Saturday slots can carry a minimum group size, which the booking flow shows for your slot. Large groups and private hire go through the events team.
