What is The Crystal Maze LIVE Experience, West End?

The Crystal Maze LIVE Experience recreates the 90s TV game show: your team runs through the Industrial, Futuristic, Medieval and Aztec zones, winning crystals in timed games, then spends them in the Crystal Dome.

The London version is on Shaftesbury Avenue, in the heart of the West End.

How does it work?

You play in a team of up to 8, taking turns on physical, mental, mystery and skill games. Every crystal you win buys time in the Dome at the end.

Standard tickets are for ages 13 and over. Family tickets, sold in school holidays, need at least three tickets including one child aged 9 to 16 and one adult.

How much is The Crystal Maze in London?

From £47 per person for a standard ticket, £52 for VIP, or from £39.50 per adult on a family ticket.

What is the food and drink like?

The Crystal Maze is an experience rather than a bar, so plan to eat before or after; Soho and Chinatown are a few minutes' walk.

How do you get there?

How do you book?

Book online through The Crystal Maze's own site. Teams are up to 8 people.
