What is Puttshack?

Puttshack is crazy golf where the ball does the scoring. Each ball carries a chip tracked by sensors under the green, so scores appear on a screen automatically rather than being written down, and the holes are built around games rather than plain obstacles.

White City has two 9-hole courses inside Westfield London, alongside a bar and restaurant and private event suites, and is one of several UK Puttshack sites alongside Bank.

How does it work?

You book a round on one of the two 9-hole courses and play as a group, with scoring tracked automatically on every hole. A round for two players typically takes about 30 minutes; larger groups take longer.

Private event suites can be booked for groups, with one, two or three-plus suites available depending on party size, up to 12 or more players.

What is the food and drink like?

Puttshack White City runs a globally-inspired food menu alongside a full cocktail bar, served throughout the venue so there's no need to leave the course to order. The full menu is on Puttshack's own food and drink page.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

Westfield London, Ariel Way, W12 7HB. Inside the Westfield shopping centre, a short walk from White City station on the Central line.

How do you book?

Book online through Puttshack's own site, choosing a course, a product and a headcount. Booking online also unlocks the £1pp discount on 9-hole rounds.

Larger groups and private hire go through Puttshack's events team, who can arrange multiple event suites for bigger parties.

Common questions

Who is Puttshack, White City good for?
Our ratings: families with kids 5/5, dates 4/5, groups of friends 4/5, work socials with colleagues 3/5. Best for families with kids: Junior rates; under-18s welcome until 7pm. Least suited to work socials with colleagues: Event suites for 12 or more.
When is the best time to go to Puttshack, White City?
Best weekend afternoons (Saturday or Sunday) (5/5): Opens 11am Sunday; unlimited golf £18. Then weekday daytime 4/5, after work 4/5, late night 1/5.
How much is Puttshack, White City?
£14 per adult for a 9-hole round, or £13 if you book online. Unlimited golf is £18 per adult, Sunday to Thursday only. Junior rate is £9.50 for a round and £16 for unlimited golf.
Is there a discount for booking online?
Yes, booking a 9-hole round online saves £1 per adult against the walk-in rate.
Does Puttshack have peak pricing?
No, the 9-hole rate is flat all week. Unlimited golf is the one product with a day restriction: Sunday to Thursday only, not Friday or Saturday.
How many courses does White City have?
Two 9-hole courses, plus private event suites bookable for groups.
Are under-18s allowed?
Yes earlier in the day if accompanied by an adult, but under-18s aren't permitted in the venue after 7pm.
