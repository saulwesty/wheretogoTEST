What is Boom Battle Bar, Wandsworth?

Boom Battle Bar is a multi-activity games bar, where every booking is a bundle of game time, drinks and street food. The Wandsworth site is at Ram Quarter, opposite Southside.

It's one of five London Boom Battle Bars: Oxford Street, Liverpool Street, The O2, Wandsworth and Ealing.

How does it work?

You book a BOOM Bundle rather than a bare game slot: one full-length game (55 minutes for most games, 30 for axe throwing), two premium drinks and either two street food plates or a burger per person.

Under-18s are welcome with an adult before 7pm; after that it's strictly 18+, with Challenge 25 ID checks. Axe throwing is 18+ at all times.

How much is Boom Battle Bar, Wandsworth?

£27.50 per person for a BOOM Bundle of one game, two drinks and food.

What is the food and drink like?

Street food and burgers come with every bundle. Outside a bundle, pints of BOOM Lager and glasses of Prosecco are £5, with 2-for-1 cocktails from 4pm to 7pm Monday to Thursday. The venue is cashless.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

9 Barley Walk, Ram Quarter, SW18 1UL. Wandsworth Town station is about ten minutes' walk; the venue is opposite TK Maxx Southside.

How do you book?

Walk-ins are welcome for the bar, but book games ahead. Bookings are non-refundable; give at least 24 hours' notice by email to reschedule.
