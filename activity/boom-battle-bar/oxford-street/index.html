What is Boom Battle Bar?

Boom Battle Bar is a multi-activity games bar. Oxford Street runs across a basement and ground floor directly opposite the Elizabeth line entrance at Tottenham Court Road, with augmented-reality axe throwing, augmented-reality darts, crazier golf, shuffleboard, beer or prosecco pong, karaoke booths and American pool.

Unlike most venues in this set, Boom doesn't sell games individually; everything is booked as part of a bundle that also includes drinks and food.

How does it work?

You book a bundle rather than a bare game slot. The standard BOOM Bundle gets you one full-length game session (55 minutes for most games, 30 minutes for axe throwing), two premium drinks including cocktails, and either two street food plates or a burger, per person.

A cheaper lunchtime version runs Monday to Friday, trading a shorter list of extras for a lower price. Games are capped to keep groups moving; large groups beyond the online booking limit need to contact the venue directly.

How much does it cost at Boom Battle Bar, Oxford Street?

£27.50 per person for the standard BOOM Bundle, or £15pp for the Monday to Friday lunchtime deal.

What is the food and drink like?

Street food and a burger option are built into every bundle, with cocktails included among the drinks. Outside a bundle, drinks include £5 pints of BOOM Lager and £5 glasses of Prosecco. The venue is cashless only.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

70–88 Oxford Street, W1D 1BP (basement and ground floor). Tottenham Court Road station is a few minutes' walk, directly opposite the Elizabeth line entrance.

How do you book?

Online bookings run for groups of up to 12; bigger groups need to contact the venue directly. A £10 per-person hold applies for no-shows or late cancellations, and it's over-18s only after 7pm, with axe throwing 18+ at all times.

Common questions

How much does it cost at Boom Battle Bar, Oxford Street?
The standard BOOM Bundle is £27.50 per person for one full-length game, two drinks and food. A cheaper lunchtime deal runs Monday to Friday at £15pp.
Can I just pay for a game without the bundle?
Boom Battle Bar doesn't publish standalone per-game pricing; bookings go through a bundle.
Is it 18+?
It's over-18s only after 7pm; before that, under-18s are welcome with an adult. Axe throwing is always 18+.
What games are on offer?
Augmented-reality axe throwing, augmented-reality darts, crazier golf, shuffleboard, beer or prosecco pong, karaoke booths and American pool.
Do I need to book?
Pre-booking is recommended for games; walk-ins are welcome for the bar itself. A £10 per-person hold applies for no-shows or late cancellations.
