What is Fairgame, Canary Wharf?

Fairgame is a fairground built for adults: classic stall games rebuilt with digital scoring, plus cocktails, street food and prizes. Canary Wharf is on Fisherman's Walk, with nine games including Alley Hoop, Final Furlong, Gopher and Lawn of the Dead.

There's a second, larger Fairgame at One New Change, by St Paul's.

How does it work?

You book a 75-minute games session and play every game in rotation, with scores tracked so your group can compete. Prime-time sessions give you two rounds of each game.

Fairgame is strictly 18+, and anyone who looks 25 or under needs physical photo ID.

How much is Fairgame, Canary Wharf?

£15 per person off-peak or £19 at prime time, for 75 minutes of games.

What is the food and drink like?

Food comes from on-site traders including Burger & Beyond, Alby's pizza and Taco Collective, with cocktails, craft beer and a candy stand.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

25-35 Fisherman's Walk, E14 4DH. Fisherman's Walk is on the waterfront a few minutes from Canary Wharf station, on the Jubilee and Elizabeth lines and the DLR.

How do you book?

Book online for groups of 1 to 15; groups of 16 or more book through the events team. Bookings are fully refundable up to 72 hours ahead, and non-refundable within 24 hours.
