What is Fairgame, City?

Fairgame is a fairground built for adults: classic stall games rebuilt with digital scoring, plus cocktails, street food and prizes. The City site is inside One New Change by St Paul's, with twelve games, among them Beaky Finders, Droppin Dimes, Havoc in the Hive and Master Blaster.

It's larger than the original Canary Wharf site, with room for 800 on an event.

How does it work?

You book a 90-minute games session and play every game in rotation, with scores tracked so your group can compete. Prime-time sessions give you two consecutive rounds of each game.

Fairgame is strictly 18+, and anyone who looks 25 or under needs physical photo ID.

How much is Fairgame, City?

£22 per person off-peak or £25 at prime time, for 90 minutes of games.

What is the food and drink like?

Food comes from on-site traders including Burger & Beyond, Alby's pizza and Le Bab, with cocktails and craft beer from the Bumper Bars and a candy stand.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

1 New Change, EC4M 9AF. Inside One New Change, a couple of minutes from St Paul's station on the Central line.

How do you book?

Book online for groups of 1 to 15. Groups of 16 or more book through the events team, from £48 per person. Bookings are fully refundable up to 72 hours ahead.

Is Fairgame adults only?

When is Fairgame, City open?
Tuesday and Wednesday 11:30am to midnight, Thursday to Saturday 11:30am to 1am, and Sunday 11:30am to midnight. It's closed on Mondays.
