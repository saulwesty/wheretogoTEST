What is Electric Shuffle?

Electric Shuffle is a shuffleboard bar where the table does the work. You slide weighted pucks down a long board and a projection system scores it, runs the games and keeps the leaderboard, so nobody has to remember the rules of traditional shuffleboard.

There are three London sites: London Bridge, King's Cross and Canary Wharf.

How does it work?

You book a table for 60 or 90 minutes and share it between your group; up to 16 people can play on one board. The semi-private area takes groups of 2 to 32, and anything from 33 to 400 goes through Electric Shuffle's events team rather than the standard online booking flow.

Food and drink are ordered at the table through a call button on the scoreboard and brought straight to you, so you don't need to leave the game to order.

18+ to play, even with an adult present. Under-18s can be in the bar area before 5pm for food, but can't play shuffleboard or stand at the boards at any time. It's worth flagging to your group before you book, since it isn't always obvious at the point of booking.

From Wednesday to Saturday, a table costs £13 per person for 60 minutes or £17 per person for 90 minutes.

  • Saturday bookings need a minimum group size of 6 or more.

What is the food and drink like?

Cocktail-led bar food, ordered at the table and brought to you while you play. Sourdough pizzas are the standout, alongside burgers and small plates. Weekend brunch is bottomless: two hours of bottomless pizza and Prosecco or beer, plus an hour of shuffleboard and live DJs.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

London Bridge. A short walk from London Bridge station, which puts the Jubilee line, the Northern line and every south-eastern mainline service within reach. It's mostly step-free with accessible toilets; one shuffleboard sits on a raised platform that isn't wheelchair accessible.

The other two sites. King's Cross for anyone arriving from the north, Canary Wharf for the finance crowd.

How do you book?

Online, choosing a site, a date, a session length and a headcount. Because the rate is set by the day rather than the hour, you can pick a time to suit the group without worrying about the price moving.

You can cancel or postpone up to 48 hours before your booking; after that, the gaming fee isn't refunded. Larger groups and full venue hire go through Electric Shuffle's events team.
