What is Electric Shuffle, Canary Wharf?

Electric Shuffle is shuffleboard with cameras over every table, projecting the games and keeping score, from the same operator as Flight Club. Canary Wharf is on Cabot Square, with ten boards and two bars.

It's one of three London Electric Shuffles, alongside London Bridge and King's Cross.

How does it work?

You book a table for 60 or 90 minutes and share it with your group; the table projects each game and scores it, so the rules explain themselves as you play. Online bookings take groups of 2 to 32, and bigger events of up to 350 go through the events team.

Players must be 18 or over to use the tables, which is Electric Shuffle's policy at all its London sites.

What is the food and drink like?

Food runs from lunch through to the evening, with cocktails from the two bars. Brunch runs Thursday to Sunday, with Prosecco and bottomless pizza, and an optional shuffleboard sampler added on.

How do you get there?

Get directions on Google Maps ↗

10 Cabot Square, E14 4EY. Cabot Square is a couple of minutes from Canary Wharf station, on the Jubilee and Elizabeth lines and the DLR.

How do you book?

Book online through Electric Shuffle's own site for groups of 2 to 32. Larger groups, up to 350, go through the events team.
