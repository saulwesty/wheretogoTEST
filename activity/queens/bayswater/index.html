What is Queens Skate Dine Bowl, Bayswater?

Queens on Queensway is home to London's oldest surviving all-year-round indoor ice rink, alongside 17 bowling lanes, six interactive darts oches and what it calls London's only on-ice curling experience.

Bowling is split between 12 main lanes with booth seating and five ruby-red VIP lanes with service from the bar and restaurant.

How does it work?

Skating, bowling, curling and darts are booked separately through Queens' booking system. Curling runs on Thursday and Friday from 7pm, with up to 50 minutes on the ice after a quick briefing.

Regular skaters can buy a membership for unlimited public sessions.

17 Queensway, W2 4QP. Queensway station, on the Central line, and Bayswater, on the Circle and District lines, are both a short walk along Queensway.

How do you book?

Book any activity online through Queens' booking system, or call 020 7229 0172.
