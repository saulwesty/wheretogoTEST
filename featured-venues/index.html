Wildgoose

Wildgoose runs team activities in London that start and end wherever your group chooses, including treasure hunts around the city streets and indoor challenges in your own office.

Right for

  • Office managers organising a team social or Christmas party.
  • Larger groups, with group sizes from 8 up to 750.
  • Teams who want to finish at their own party venue or a pub of their choice.

What can you book in London?

Wildgoose is not a single venue with an address, so each activity takes place wherever your group is meeting. There are three to choose from.

The Ultimate

This is Wildgoose’s all-time favourite, and it works all year round. Teams explore the streets around wherever they are meeting, with trivia, photo challenges and competitive tasks.

  • Time90 to 120 minutes
  • Group size8 to 750 people
  • PriceFrom £15 + VAT per person

Outdoor Christmas treasure hunt

Festive Explorer

A GPS Christmas treasure hunt around London, with or without pub stops along the way. Teams can finish wherever the party is happening, and groups can run it themselves or add a remote host or an onsite host.

  • Time2 hours
  • Group size8 to 750 people
  • PriceFrom £15 + VAT per person

Indoor team challenges

Level Up

These are fast-paced team challenges for groups who would rather stay inside. Level Up runs in your own office or at a venue you have already booked, and there is no equipment to set up.

  • Time60 to 90 minutes
  • Group size8 to 750 people
  • PriceFrom £12 + VAT per person

Is there a Christmas discount?

10% off

Any Christmas activity, with the code

For bookings made by 23:59 on 31 October 2026.

You can book for a minimum number of people to secure your December date and time, then increase the numbers nearer the time. The extra places get the same 10% discount.

Why do we recommend Wildgoose?

  • Everyone can get involved, from small teams to whole companies of up to 750.
  • It works in your area. Every activity starts and ends wherever your group chooses.
  • It’s great for team building, with trivia, photo challenges and competitive tasks.

What is Wildgoose’s track record?

  • Running team experiences since 2004
  • Rated 5.0 on Trustpilot from 665 reviews
  • Has delivered for teams at Google, Amazon and monday.com

How do you book?

You can book online, often with as little as 48 hours’ notice. Every activity can start and end wherever your group chooses.
