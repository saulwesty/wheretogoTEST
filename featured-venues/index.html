Wild Goose

Photo to come

Wild Goose runs team activities in London that start and end wherever your group chooses, including treasure hunts around the city streets and indoor challenges in your own office. Prices start at £12 per person plus VAT, and groups can range from 8 to 750 people.

See Wild Goose London activities

Why we think they are a great venue:

  • Every activity can start and end wherever your group chooses, so you are not tied to one location.
  • Groups of 8 to 750 people can take part, which covers both a small team and a whole company.
  • There are indoor and outdoor options, so the weather does not have to decide your plans.
  • For Christmas, you can reserve your date with a minimum number of people and add more later at the same discount.
  • You can book online, often with as little as 48 hours' notice, according to Wild Goose.

Who is Wild Goose?

Wild Goose is a team-building company that says it has been running experiences for teams since 2004. It also says it is rated 5.0 on Trustpilot from 665 reviews. Both figures come from Wild Goose, and we have not yet checked them ourselves.

Wild Goose is not a single venue with an address, because each activity takes place wherever your group is meeting. That makes it a different kind of listing from most of the venues on Where To Go London.

What can you book in London?

The Ultimate is an outdoor GPS treasure hunt that Wild Goose describes as its all-time favourite. Teams explore the streets around wherever they are meeting, with trivia, photo challenges and competitive tasks, and it takes 90 to 120 minutes. It runs all year round.

Festive Explorer is an outdoor Christmas version of the treasure hunt, with or without pub stops along the way. Teams can finish wherever the party is happening, and it takes 2 hours. Groups can run it themselves or add a remote host or an onsite host.

Level Up is an indoor option made up of fast-paced team challenges, and it takes 60 to 90 minutes. It runs in your own office or at a venue you have already booked, and no equipment needs setting up.

Is there a Christmas discount?

Wild Goose is offering 10% off any Christmas activity with the code WHATTODO10 for bookings made by 23:59 on 31 October 2026. You can book for a minimum number of people to secure your December date and time, then increase the numbers nearer the time, and the extra places get the same 10% discount.

How do you book?

You can book online, and Wild Goose says this is often possible with as little as 48 hours' notice. Every activity can start and end wherever your group chooses.

Is Wild Goose right for your group?

Wild Goose suits teams who want to organise an activity around a date, a place and a headcount they already have, rather than fit around a fixed venue.

Right for

  • Office managers organising a team social or Christmas party.
  • Larger groups, with group sizes from 8 up to 750.
  • Teams who want to finish at their own party venue or a pub of their choice.
