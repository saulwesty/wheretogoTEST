What is Where To Go?

Where To Go is a guide to London's competitive socialising venues: darts, bowling, crazy golf, shuffleboard, cricket and football simulators, bingo and similar. It's built for people organising a group outing, whether that's an office social, a birthday or a stag or hen do, and covers each venue on its own terms rather than ranking them against each other.
