How the tool decides

The ranking is cost per person for your group, cheapest first, using the band we sampled for the slot you picked. Where a venue charges an admission fee on top of the game price, that fee is already in the number.

A venue is dropped from the results when its own rules would stop your booking. Flight Club needs a minimum of six players on Friday evenings and all day Saturday at every site except Islington, so a group of four asking for a Friday sees Islington only. Toca Social advises four or more, so a pair is warned rather than dropped.

Bounce appears without a price because we have not sampled its booking flow yet. We would rather show you the gap than fill it with a number from a press release.

Common questions

What is the cheapest activity in London for a big group?
For groups above about ten, Sixes Cricket is the best value per minute in this set, because session length scales with group size at the same rate per head. Twelve people get 90 minutes for what a pair pays for 30. Rowans is the cheapest headline price at £9.60 per game, but the Friday and Saturday admission fee of £5 per person changes the maths after 19:00.
Do these prices include food and drink?
No. Every figure on this site is for the activity only. Food and drink are charged on top at every venue here.
Which venues will take a group of twelve or more?
Sixes Cricket handles up to 30 across two nets, Toca Social takes up to 24 through the booking flow, and Flight Club takes up to 36. Above those numbers most venues route you to an events team, and none of them publish those rates.
Why does the same venue cost double at different times?
Several of these venues price by demand. Toca Social runs from £10 to £23 for an identical 45 minutes depending on the hour. Puttshack charges the same all week. Knowing which venue does which is most of the saving available.
